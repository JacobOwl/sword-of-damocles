# Rule switches: what each one does to the stories (2026-11-22)

Headless runs only, no human play. Each number is from 500 seeds (seeds 1 to 500) for each player, balance package and set of switches. Rerun with `npm run sim -- --seeds 500 --switch-sweep --tunings standard` (or `reapWhatYouSow`); add `--bots careful,patron` to pick players.

**Nothing in the default game changed.** All five switches are off unless you tick them on the start screen. A test checks that with every switch off, the game plays as before (the standard digest was re-pinned only for two text fixes, see the end).

Not tuned to history: no number here was set to match how often real tyrants fell.

## The five switches

| Switch | What it does | Research |
|---|---|---|
| **Warnings are clues** | Each warning tells of one thing the plotters really did: who began it and on what grievance, who joined and why, who was sounded out and said no (he comes and tells you), which faction or the guard paid in, who is buying swords. Whispers give no faction; Rumors and Named do. Plot cards list "What you have heard". | proposal 1 |
| **Blows can miss** | A strike that beats your defense by 1 or 2 wounds you (you lose an action next season; the leader flees and every plotter is named). By 3 or 4 you live, but the leader takes one of your offices by force and your house is plundered. Only a margin of 5 or more kills or exiles you. | smallest form of proposal 3 |
| **People escalate** | A man asks for an office once. Refused, he waits a season and returns with a demand, in public. Refuse the demand and he never asks for it again: he holds a grudge and acts (and if he leads a faction, it is shamed with him). A man who was your enemy before comes back a rank higher. | proposal 5 |
| **A Pretender needs a coalition** | A political rival rises to Pretender only while a second faction, not his own, is Angry or worse with you; while none is, his bid stalls (said once). | sweep problem 1 |
| **Chronicle as story** | The chronicle is told by who each season was about, counts repeats ("the third time"), says where each turning point began, and ends with each person's thread through the reign and "How it ended". No rules change. | proposal 2 |

## The short version

1. **People escalate fixes the repetition.** The most times one man asks you for the same office in a game falls from **8 to 17** to about **2**. In long reigns (Reap what you sow, patron) the same man became your enemy **3.9** times at most; with the switch, **1.7**. People made your enemy more than twice: **0.88 a game → 0.07**. Returning enemies come back stronger, so Nemeses rise a little (0.12 → 0.20 for the patron). Survival barely moves (careful 67% → 65% under Standard).
2. **The coalition switch breaks Nicoteles' road, for players who keep factions content.** Patron under Reap what you sow: deposed **11% → 3%**, and Nicoteles' share of the reigns that fall **72% → 42%**. Under Standard: deposed **27% → 10%**. It does nothing for the simple bot, which lets every faction go Angry, so a second faction is always there: that is the switch working as written.
3. **Blows can miss halves the chance that a strike kills you** (about 90% → 40 to 60%) and turns many endings into wounds. Careful under Standard: decade survival **67% → 80%**, killed or exiled **26% → 13%**. The iron hand gains most (Standard 3% → 9%). Cost: players who now survive a blow are later voted out more often (simple bot deposed 42% → 58%, Nicoteles over half of those games).
4. **Warnings are clues** changes no balance (survival within 1 point), but the share of warning lines that are new in a game rises from about **25 to 40%** to **45 to 65%**, and every one is true. The careful bot reads them (a grievance or faction you were told of raises a suspect; a man who came to say no is cleared); that only nudges "leader known" (97% → 98%), because bots already found leaders well.
5. **Chronicle as story** changes no rule. The engine chronicle's most-repeated sentence falls from 7 to 20 a game to about 1, but read that with care: repeats are now counted ("the third time") instead of written out, so the measure partly counts the rewrite, not fewer repeated deeds. Escalation is what removes repeated deeds.
6. **All five together** (Standard): careful 80%, patron 76%, iron hand 9%, simple 1% survive the decade; no crash or broken state in 28,000 games.

## Recommendation (Jacob decides)

- **Turn on People escalate and Warnings are clues for the next playtest.** Both fix things you named (the same petition every season, warnings that said nothing), and neither changes balance much.
- **Try A Pretender needs a coalition** if Nicoteles keeps ending your reigns. It gives a clear counter (keep one other faction above Angry), and it doesn't nerf him by hand.
- **Try Blows can miss with Standard balance, not with Reap what you sow.** With Reap the careful player already lasts 87% of decades, and 90% is too safe. With Standard it turns early deaths into wounds and lost offices, which read better, while the iron hand still mostly dies.
- **Chronicle as story:** turn it on and read one finished game. It is the cheapest test of the question "is this a story you would retell?".

The design council's "first island test" preset (Reap what you sow, clues and blows can miss on) is close to this; my one difference is the warning above about Reap plus Blows can miss being easy for a careful player.

## Problems still open

- **Nicoteles still ends over half of the simple bot's games** (Standard 42% → 56% with everything on, Reap 56% → 68%). The simple bot lets every faction go Angry, so the coalition never holds him back. Not a fix for a careless ruler, by design.
- **Wounds favour the deposer.** Surviving a blow keeps a weak ruler alive long enough for the assembly to vote him out.
- **Offices taken by force are rare** (0.00 to 0.07 a game): the margin of 3 or 4 is uncommon, and the leader must be inside the walls. Most partial blows are wounds.
- **Clues help bots little.** A person reading them may do better; only a playtest can say.
- **Political rivals still flicker** (11 to 15 "becomes a political rival" lines a game for careful and patron players). Not touched by any switch.

## Bugs fixed (on by default, not switches)

- The Watch result said "Theodorus is in a plot against you, led by Theodorus" when a man led his own plot; it now says "and leads it himself".
- The Take his place ending listed all four ways you can lose before saying the one that happened; it now says only the one.
- **Not reproduced:** "exiled men arrested again" (seen once in seed 7 before the events system). In 1,000 games (five players, 200 seeds each), no one outside the walls was taken except by capture at Aetna, which is the rule.
- The two text fixes change the standard digest. With those two lines put back, the old digest still matches, so the switches change nothing while off. The note is in `tests/balance.test.ts`.
