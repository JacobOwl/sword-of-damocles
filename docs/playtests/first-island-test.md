# First island test: what to watch (for Jacob)

Play it at the usual link. "First island test" is the first choice on the start screen and is already selected; press Begin.

**What it turns on:** the island game; Reap what you sow balance; Warnings are clues, Blows can miss, Chronicle as story, Beaten armies can break. Standard stays the default for everything else. (Source: `data/presets.json`, chosen by the assumptions council, `docs/council/2026-11-23-assumptions-council.md`.)

**At the end of season 10** the game asks five short questions (what you'd retell, what confused you, what felt unfair, too easy / about right / too hard, anything else). Then play on as long as you like.

**When you stop**, press "Copy my game" at the foot of the page and paste the line into the project chat. It holds your seed, your setup, every move and your answers, so a thread can replay exactly what you saw (`npm run replay-save -- <file>`), including the hidden rolls.

## Things to watch for
- Did ten seasons give you a story you would tell someone? Which moment?
- When a plot came for you, could you see it coming, and from whom? Did any blow feel random?
- Did the island map matter, or did you play only the court? Did a town change sides, and did you know why?
- Did Carthage feel like a power with its own goals, or like a timer?
- Was any screen, word or number confusing? Note where.
