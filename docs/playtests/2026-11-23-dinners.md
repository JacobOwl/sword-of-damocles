## Counts, 100 seeds each, the simple bot

The bot gives few dinners. It gives one only when it has 3 silver to spare, and it ranks dinners below its other choices. So read the per-dinner rates, not the per-game ones. A player who dines every season will see about four to ten times as much.

Per dinner (citadel): about 2.4 moments, 0.36 new friendships, 0.17 quarrels, 0.2 secrets let slip, and 0.8 choices put to you. The couch changed hands at most dinners, because the bot honours whoever likes it best that season, and almost half of those changes (17 of 39) left a man slighted.

Nothing looks broken. There is no dinner in which everything happens, and no run of dinners in which nothing does. Quarrels are rare at "mixed as usual" (the bot's usual wine), as designed. Friends from your table helped a recruit roll only 3 times in 100 games, because few bot dinners mean few friendships. Watch this number if dinners become common.

## Three sample dinners (citadel, the simple bot, read straight from the log)

These are the busiest dinners in the first 40 seeds, one per seed. Each line is a log entry with its reason in brackets.

### Seed 2, Winter 405/404 BC

- Silver -1 (now 2). (the dinner)
- You give a dinner. On the couch beside yours, Doricus; with you, the brother of Demarchus, Damon the smith, Nicoteles. The wine is mixed in the usual way. (Give a dinner)
- Doricus now reclines on the couch beside yours at your dinners: it adds to Doricus's standing. (you seated him beside you)
- Doricus's opinion of you rises to +3. (the couch beside yours at dinner)
- During the meal: Your guards stop the brother of Demarchus at the door: no one comes near you unsearched. (an enemy invited to your table)
- The brother of Demarchus waits at the door between your guards, and they look to you. (an enemy at your table)
- During the libations: Nicoteles and Damon the smith both want the same office, and both know it now. The talk between their couches turns cold, then ugly. (two men who want the same thing)
- Nicoteles and Damon the smith are at odds now. (they want the same office)
- During the drinking: The wine works on you too. You say what you really think of Hipparinus, who is not at the table. The brother of Demarchus remembers every word. (the wine, and a host who drank it)
- Hipparinus's opinion of you falls to 0. (word reached Hipparinus of what you said at dinner)

### Seed 3, Winter 404/403 BC

- Silver -1 (now 2). (the dinner)
- You give a dinner. On the couch beside yours, Doricus; with you, Theodorus, the brother of Demarchus, Leptines. The wine is mixed in the usual way. (Give a dinner)
- Doricus now reclines on the couch beside yours at your dinners: it adds to Doricus's standing. (you seated him beside you)
- Doricus's opinion of you rises to +3. (the couch beside yours at dinner)
- During the meal: The brother of Demarchus and Theodorus share a cup and talk late of their families. They leave as friends. (guests of one faction at one table)
- The brother of Demarchus and Theodorus are friends now. (they shared a cup at your table)
- During the libations: Doricus rises on the couch beside yours and pours the libation to your health, before the whole table. (the place of honour, given and taken well)
- During the drinking: Doricus mocks Theodorus before the whole table, and Theodorus answers in kind. Everyone looks at you. (strong talk between men of different factions)
- Doricus and Theodorus are at odds now. (Doricus mocked Theodorus at your table)
- Doricus has mocked Theodorus before your guests, and Theodorus has answered. The table waits for you. (a quarrel at your dinner)

### Seed 8, Winter 403/402 BC

- Silver -1 (now 2). (the dinner)
- You give a dinner. On the couch beside yours, Heloris; with you, Leptines, the leader of the exiles at Aetna, Theodorus. The wine is mixed in the usual way. (Give a dinner)
- During the meal: Your guards stop Leptines at the door: no one comes near you unsearched. (an enemy invited to your table)
- Leptines waits at the door between your guards, and they look to you. (an enemy at your table)
- During the libations: Theodorus and Leptines, seated near each other, clasp hands over the bowl and put their old quarrel aside. (an old quarrel, and wine enough to forgive it)
- Theodorus and Leptines have put their quarrel aside. (they made peace at your table)
- During the drinking: Theodorus, pleased with the wine, tells a story about Leptines that was never meant to be told. You hear it too. (a story told across the couches)
- You now hold a secret over Leptines (a story told at your dinner). (Theodorus told it at your table)

**Reading them.** Seed 2 has a door, a rivalry and a careless host in one evening. Seed 3 seats a friend on the couch, and the same friend then mocks another guest and starts a quarrel. Seed 8 pays off an earlier quarrel: the two men make peace, and one tells a story about the other. In all three, the bot left the choices unanswered, so the default choices were taken.
