# Three-lens review of the first island test build (2026-11-23)

Report only. Three lenses: UI designer and artist, historian and game designer, new player. Checked at 1280 and 390 pixels wide in Chromium, on the "First island test" preset (careful bot saves cut at seasons 1, 10 and 21, seeds 2, 5 and 19): start screen, first-season guide, season screen, island map, court, dangers, chronicle, the season-10 verdict and the Send the army panel.

Each item is marked **cheap** (an hour or less, no rule change) or **costly**. The top five are starred. The five starred items were fixed before the freeze, because they were cheap and they were plain breakages, not taste. Nothing else was changed. Jacob picks which of the rest, if any, get done after he plays.

## Starred: fixed before the freeze

1. ★ **cheap** (new player). The season news said "He did not forget: You threw him in chains." The man was not named and "You" had a capital mid-sentence. Now it reads "<his name> has not forgotten: you threw him in chains, <season>." The story chronicle already named him; the news now does too.
2. ★ **cheap** (UI). Carthage's council reason repeated the same bracket for each bloc: "the Elders (Carthage's anger is 8) and the Hundred (Carthage's anger is 8) and the people (…)". Now blocs that agree share one reason: "the Elders, the Hundred and the people of Carthage, since Carthage's anger is 8; decided by the council".
3. ★ **cheap** (new player). Send the army showed the battle forecast but kept the button disabled until you also chose "Orders". "Spare it" (or "Simply march there") is now chosen for you; you can still change it to sack.
4. ★ **cheap** (game designer). The "This season" line told you where Carthage's army was but not where yours was. It now adds "Your army is at Gela, 12 days from it."
5. ★ **cheap** (UI). Two plots at the same stage both read "A plot, source unknown". The second now reads "A second plot, source unknown".

These change the wording of the log, so the two pinned digests were re-pinned. The 100-seed summaries (Standard and the preset) are identical before and after: only words changed.

## Not done: everything else seen

### UI designer and artist

- **costly** At 390 the ledger (silver, grain, guard, factions) fills about half the first screen before the season's choices. Collapsing it behind one line would help phones; Jacob judges on a computer, so it waits.
- **costly / taste** The season screen runs about 1,800 words at 1280 when every panel is open. It reads as a catalogue page, which fits the look, but a new player scrolls a lot. Candidate cut: fold the faction notes by default.
- **cheap** Island events sit mid-column on the map view at 1280; at the top they would be seen first.
- **cheap** The masthead says "Season 11 of 40" just after the season-10 verdict. "Season 11" alone would not suggest the test runs to 40.
- **cheap** The court rank words (Malcontent, Contender, Pretender) are explained only in a hover tip, which a phone player may never open.
- **cheap** The "Also:" part of the "This season" line starts its items with capitals after a semicolon ("Also: Your army…; Syracuse has…"). Readable, but untidy.

### Historian and game designer

- History and invention tags checked on the island data: `data/island.json` 9 history, 2 invention; `data/carthage.json` 3 history, 26 invention (the council blocs are invention from Aristotle, Politics 1273a, written two generations later, and say so); `data/island-events.json` 31 history, 15 invention. Every history event's cited passage appears in a card under `docs/research/`. No gaps found.
- **cheap** Carthage's council reason shows the anger number ("Carthage's anger is 8"). It is a game meter, not an id, so it is allowed, but "Carthage is angry, close to war" would read more like a chronicle.
- **costly** Plots still rarely strike inside ten seasons (see the ten-season report). That is a pacing question for Jacob after he plays, not a freeze fix, and not to be tuned to history.
- **costly** The opening is the same every seed (same cast, same first offers). The sourced peacetime events from stream 3 vary the map, but the court opening does not vary much.

### New player

- **cheap** The island's first-season guide does not mention the season-10 verdict, or that you can play on after it. Only the preset card on the start screen says so.
