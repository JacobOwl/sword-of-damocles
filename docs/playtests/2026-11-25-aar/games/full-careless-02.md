Carthage's envoys come to Syracuse with terms: peace, and each side keeps what it holds (the seventh time).
Meanwhile, again, Leptines courts the guard with gifts from the treasury. You come home. Leptines escapes over the walls. Syracuse is yours again.
You learn that Nicoteles writes to friends in Corinth for men and money.

### Winter 397/396 BC

The guard goes unpaid. They are owed 27 silver. The men on the great fleet go unpaid and down their tools. Again, Later word from Carthage: the story that the Libyan villages were ready to rise was false. The treasury is empty. You could tell the assembly that Demeter has appeared to you and asked for the women's ornaments to be brought to her temple, as your own household has already done. A trader brings a quiet word from Maharbal, head of one of Carthage's great merchant houses: spare Carthage's merchant ships, and he will speak for peace among the Elders (the third time). The citizens riot for bread (the seventh time). The guard has gone two seasons unpaid (the third time). Your mercenaries no longer look you in the eye (the third time). Among the families, Adranodorus son of Hippocrates, of Catana died.
In the same season, Thearides petitions you for the treasury. Again, At your council, Thearides says the captain of the guard is plotting against you. Thearides's petition goes unanswered (the seventh time). Thearides leaves without a word. He will be back for the treasury.
In a brawl in the agora, one of your mercenaries has killed a citizen. A crowd wants him handed over. The captain of the guard wants him protected.
You send Aristotle the Spartan into exile.
Your guards take Damon the smith.
Doricus takes the seat of the sword at your council.
A convoy sails from the grain ports of Italy for Syracuse with 2 months of grain (the fourth time). Again, Blood in the agora: you chose to hand the mercenary to the citizens' court. Demeter's ornaments: you chose to leave the gods out of it. A rumor from Carthage: a merchant's word: you chose to seize his ships anyway (the third time). Messana sends your envoys home with nothing.

### Spring 396 BC

The guard goes unpaid. They are owed 29 silver. At the festival a poet recites verses that mock you. The crowd laughs, then looks to see what you will do. Again, a merchant ship heavy with Egyptian grain puts in at the Great Harbour, asking a high price. A Greek trader back from Libya says the farmers there curse Carthage's levies and dues (the third time). Carthage's envoys come to Syracuse with terms: peace, and each side keeps what it holds (the eighth time). Among the families, Megacles son of Adranodorus, of Catana died.
Elsewhere, Thearides gathers a following.
You use what you know: Andron still nurses a grudge (you passed him over for the treasury) and talks of it in private. That secret is spent. You make it clear to Andron that he should do nothing for now.
Your guards take Hipparinus. You have Hipparinus put to death.
Aristomache swears that Hipparinus's death will not be forgotten. The feud passes to him. Aristomache becomes your rival (Bereaved). It began in Summer 400 BC: Aristomache holds a grudge: you seized his estates.
A poet's verses: you chose this: Laugh, and pay him to write better verses about you. Again, a grain ship from Egypt: you chose to let it sail on. A rumor from Libya: you chose to leave Libya alone (the third time).

### Summer 396 BC

The guard goes unpaid. They are owed 31 silver. The tribute of 3 Sicilian towns reaches Carthage (the fifth time). Again, Later word from Carthage confirms the story that Maharbal would speak for peace. Among the families, Athenagoras son of Diocles, of Gela died.
At court, Thearides demands the treasury, in front of the family. He asked once already. Thearides's petition goes unanswered (the eighth time). Thearides will not ask you for the treasury again. Thearides, who sat at your council as the kinsman, is named as a plotter. His seat is empty. Again, Thearides takes the seat of the kinsman at your council.
A fever has taken hold of Nicoteles. The physicians shake their heads. Nicoteles dies of the fever.
Again, At your council, the captain of the guard says Thearides is plotting against you.
You feast Biton, Thearides, Doricus. Biton takes the seat of the sword at your council.
You send Doricus into exile.
Again, a fever: you chose to leave it to the gods. The rumor is traced back to you.

### Autumn 396 BC

The guard goes unpaid. They are owed 33 silver. Later word from Carthage confirms the story that the Libyan villages were ready to rise. Again, Danger is close, and your friends gather in the Island to counsel you. It may be time to think not of how to win, but of how this ends. Carthage's envoys come to Syracuse with terms: peace, and each side keeps what it holds (the ninth time).
Meanwhile, again, Thearides gathers a following. At your council, Thearides says the captain of the guard is plotting against you (the third time).
You send Biton into exile.
The plot strikes. Rolled 2 +7 the plot's strength = 9, against your defense of 4. It must beat your defense. Again, the plotters seize the Island. You escape with your life and go into exile. Leptines led them. Leptines rises to rank 4, Nemesis. Leptines is now your Nemesis, and works openly toward your fall.
You are not finished. You flee to Rhegium, across the strait with the captain of the guard. You carry 1 talent of silver, and 1 company of hired men follow you. Leptines holds Syracuse now.
Again, Counsel in the Island: you chose to ask the city's leave to sail away with five ships, and wait. Again, the city believes the tyranny is ending. Men drift home to their fields. You open the granaries to the people. You strike coin at the mint.

### Winter 396/395 BC

Again, a Carthaginian merchant calls at your lodging in Rhegium, across the strait. His masters, he says, would rather see you in Syracuse than Leptines. He has silver with him.
In the same season, again, Leptines puts trusted men in the Island and the dockyards.
A Carthaginian at the door: you chose to take the silver (the third time). Suits of armour are bought and hidden, to be handed out to the citizens when you come (the third time). Sparta sends silver and a captain with a company (the third time).

### Spring 395 BC

Carthage's envoys come to Syracuse with terms: peace, and each side keeps what it holds (the tenth time).
Elsewhere, again, Leptines sends envoys to you at Rhegium, across the strait with terms. Again, Leptines's envoys come to Rhegium, across the strait. Come home as a private man, they say, with a pledged word that you will be borne no enmity. Some of those with you have wives and children in the city.
Again, Terms from the city: you chose this: Refuse, and pray the gods grant him the same favour. Again, you send the envoys home with a curse. Those of your people with families in the city take the offer without you. Your men come back from Carthage's country with cattle, slaves and silver (the ninth time). A Persian satrap's agent pays you a great sum, as Pharnabazus paid Hermocrates in his exile (the third time).

### Summer 395 BC

The tribute of 3 Sicilian towns reaches Carthage (the sixth time). A captain of hired men, out of work since the peace, offers his company to you at Rhegium, across the strait for two talents (the third time).
At court, again, Aristomache will not commit to you.
A captain offers his company: you chose to hire the company (the third time). Your men come back from Carthage's country with cattle, slaves and silver (the tenth time).
