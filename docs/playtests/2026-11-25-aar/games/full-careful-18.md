**Leptines.** Autumn 405 BC: Leptines becomes a political rival (Heir). He wants your place, not your life. Winter 403/402 BC: Leptines petitions you for the generalship. Summer 402 BC: Leptines will not ask you for the generalship again. Spring 401 BC: Leptines will not ask you for the mint again. Winter 401/400 BC: Leptines will not ask you for the assembly again. Autumn 400 BC: Leptines holds a grudge: you passed him over for the generalship. Leptines ends in your prison.
**the brother of Demarchus the Returned.** Spring 404 BC: The brother of Demarchus is in a plot against you, and leads it himself. Spring 404 BC: The brother of Demarchus slips through your guards and flees the city. Summer 403 BC: You learn who plots against you: the brother of Demarchus; the leader of the exiles at Aetna; Hipparinus. Spring 402 BC: The brother of Demarchus rises to rank 4, Nemesis. Spring 402 BC: The brother of Demarchus is now your Nemesis, and works openly toward your fall. Spring 402 BC: Your guards take the brother of Demarchus. The brother of Demarchus ends in your prison.
**Theodorus.** Spring 400 BC: Theodorus petitions you for the generalship. Spring 400 BC: Theodorus leaves without a word. He will be back for the generalship. Spring 398 BC: Theodorus will not ask you for the assembly again. Autumn 398 BC: Theodorus petitions you for the treasury. Autumn 398 BC: Theodorus leaves without a word. He will be back for the treasury.
**the leader of the exiles at Aetna the Escaped.** Winter 405/404 BC: Your men stop a messenger on the road from the leader of the exiles at Aetna. He carries a sealed letter for the brother of Demarchus. Spring 404 BC: You could bury the dead of both sides with honour, and send envoys to the exiles at Aetna pledging that you bear them no enmity if they come home. Winter 400/399 BC: The leader of the exiles at Aetna holds a grudge: you exposed his secret. Winter 399/398 BC: The leader of the exiles at Aetna tells the city that you paid Nicoteles' servants to spy on him. The leader of the exiles at Aetna ends outside the walls.
**Thearides.** Spring 400 BC: Thearides petitions you for the fleet. Spring 400 BC: Thearides leaves without a word. He will be back for the fleet. Autumn 400 BC: Thearides demands the fleet, in front of the family. He asked once already. Autumn 400 BC: You grant the fleet to Thearides.
