A trader brings a quiet word from Carthalo, head of one of Carthage's great merchant houses: spare Carthage's merchant ships, and he will speak for peace among the Elders. Plague breaks out in Carthage and in Libya. Carthage remits a year's dues in Libya. The guard sells itself to a plot against you.
At court, Thearides joins the brother of Demarchus's plot (you passed him over for the fleet).
Over dinner Theodorus praises your wealth and your power, and swears there was never a man more fortunate than you. You could offer to let Theodorus taste that fortune for a day. A sword on a horse-hair: you chose to seat Theodorus on your golden couch, with a sword hung over the couch by a single horse-hair. The boys serve, the tables are piled high, and Theodorus feels fortunate until looking up. After that the food goes untouched, the garlands slip, and Theodorus begs to be let go, no longer wanting to be fortunate. The court tells the story for years. Theodorus speaks for you among the rich families.
Again, At your council, the leader of the exiles at Aetna says Theste is plotting against you.
Philistus takes offence at your offer. You pardon Philistus.
A rumor from Carthage: a merchant's word: you chose to seize his ships anyway.

### Autumn 402 BC

The guard goes unpaid. They are owed 14 silver. Libya is quiet again. Carthage's council turns to a new goal: Peace at a price. Carthage wants out of the war. Carthage's envoys come to Syracuse with terms: peace, and each side keeps what it holds. You may accept this season or the next. Carthage makes offerings to win back the gods.
Meanwhile, Hipparinus completes a step of Your fall: Buy a gate.
Philistus joins the brother of Demarchus's plot (you seized his estates). Philistus reminds you, politely and then less politely, that you owe him.
At your council, Theodorus says Theste is plotting against you (the third time). Your men empty the strongrooms of Theodorus.
Again, Theste speaks for you among the family.
Again, a debt called in: you chose to repay him with a voice at court instead. Your spies find that Hippocrates son of Timaeus, of Catana hides nothing you could use.

### Winter 402/401 BC

Later word from Carthage: the story that Carthalo would speak for peace was false. The citizens riot for bread (the fourth time). Carthage's ships trade along the coasts (the fourth time).
In the same season, stories about you spread among the rich families (the seventh time).
Again, Thearides wins friends in the guard. Your watchers follow Thearides. Thearides is in a plot against you, led by the brother of Demarchus. You learn that Thearides is sworn into the brother of Demarchus' plot against you. It is a deadly secret: a hold you can use again and again. Thearides takes the seat of the kinsman at your council.
In a brawl in the agora, one of your mercenaries has killed a citizen. A crowd wants him handed over. The captain of the guard wants him protected.
You send Theste into exile. Theste becomes your rival (Exiled). It began in Spring 404 BC: a story goes round that sets Theste and the leader of the exiles at Aetna against each other.
Again, a convoy sails from the grain ports of Italy for Syracuse with 2 months of grain. It is due next season. Again, Blood in the agora: you chose to hand the mercenary to the citizens' court.

### Spring 401 BC

The guard goes unpaid. They are owed 16 silver. A winter storm sinks the grain convoy from the grain ports of Italy. Again, Carthage's envoys come to Syracuse with terms: peace, and each side keeps what it holds. You may accept this season or the next.
Elsewhere, stories about you spread among the rich families (the eighth time).
Andron, who keeps the fortress and the treasure on the Island, comes to you at last. The leader of the exiles at Aetna urged him to seize both while your back was turned. He confesses every detail, but he did not come to you at once. You send Andron into exile.
At your council, Thearides says Damon the smith is plotting against you. Thearides, who sat at your council as the kinsman, is named as a plotter. His seat is empty. Again, Thearides takes the seat of the kinsman at your council.
At your council, Theodorus says Damon the smith is plotting against you.
At your council, the leader of the exiles at Aetna says Damon the smith is plotting against you.
You grant the treasury to Aristomache.
The keeper's confession: you chose to say nothing, and have both watched. You stop the work on the arms workshops. What is built stands, half-done.

### Summer 401 BC

The guard goes unpaid. They are owed 18 silver. The tribute of 2 Sicilian towns reaches Carthage (the fourth time). The citizens complete a step of The people's assembly: Recall a general. The plague in Carthage burns itself out.
At court, Hipparinus completes a step of Your fall: Choose the night. Hipparinus completes Your fall. Their plot gains +2 strength for good. Your men stop a messenger on the road from Hipparinus. He carries a sealed letter for Philistus.
Theodorus petitions you for the generalship. Theodorus takes offence at your offer. Theodorus's petition goes unanswered. Theodorus leaves without a word. He will be back for the generalship.
Thearides demands the fleet, in front of the family. He asked once already. Again, Thearides's petition goes unanswered. Thearides will not ask you for the fleet again. It began in Spring 403 BC: Thearides becomes a political rival (Heir). Again, Thearides, who sat at your council as the kinsman, is named as a plotter. His seat is empty. Thearides takes the seat of the kinsman at your council (the third time).
Philistus notices the broken seal.
A letter from the exiles: you chose to read it, seal it again, and send it on. You strike coin at the mint.

### Autumn 401 BC

Again, a Greek trader back from Libya says the farmers there curse Carthage's levies and dues. A little silver, he says, would set the villages burning. Again, Carthage's council turns to a new goal: War on the tyrant. Carthage means to break Syracuse. The council gives Magon sole command of the war. Carthage sends men with silver to hire soldiers abroad (the third time).
Meanwhile, Thearides petitions you for the mint. A story goes round that sets Thearides and Theodorus against each other. Thearides's standing suffers. You grant the generalship to Thearides. You grant Thearides's petition. Thearides is bound to you by office, but his influence grows. Thearides, who sat at your council as the kinsman, is named as a plotter (the third time). Thearides takes the seat of the kinsman at your council (the fourth time).
Theodorus gathers a following.
Your watchers report that a Carthaginian merchant has been carrying silver to the brother of Demarchus's house by night.
You give Doricus the seat of the sword at your council.
Carthaginian silver: you chose to let it go on, and feed him false news. Carthage acts on your lies. Again, a rumor from Libya: you chose to leave Libya alone.

### Winter 401/400 BC

The guard goes unpaid. They are owed 20 silver. Again, a storm drives ships onto the rocks off Plemmyrium. The citizens riot for bread (the fifth time). Again, Carthage's council turns to a new goal: Peace at a price. Carthage wants out of the war. Carthage's envoys come to Syracuse with terms: peace, and each side keeps what it holds (the third time). Carthage's ships trade along the coasts (the fifth time).
In the same season, again, Thearides petitions you for the mint. Again, At your council, Thearides says Damon the smith is plotting against you. Thearides's petition goes unanswered (the third time). Thearides leaves without a word. He will be back for the mint.
Again, Theodorus gathers a following. Again, At your council, Theodorus says Damon the smith is plotting against you. Theodorus slips through your guards and flees the city.
Again, the captain of the guard has lost heavily at dice, and creditors are at the door. He hints that there are men in the city who would pay those debts.
You send Aristomache into exile.
You give the leader of the exiles at Aetna the seat of the kinsman at your council.
The plot strikes. Rolled 6 +8 the plot's strength = 14, against your defense of 4. It must beat your defense. The sword falls. You are killed by men you trusted. Hipparinus led them.
A convoy sails from the grain ports of Italy for Syracuse with 2 months of grain (the third time). Again, the captain's dice: you chose to let him sort it out himself. Again, a storm off Plemmyrium: you chose to the fleet will have to wait.
