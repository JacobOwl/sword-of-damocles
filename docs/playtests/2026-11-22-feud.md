# The feud record switch (2026-11-22)

Headless runs only. 500 seeds (1 to 500) for each player, balance package and set of switches. Rerun with `npm run sim -- --seeds 500 --switch-sweep --sets none,escalation,feud,escalation+feud,all --tunings standard` (or `reapWhatYouSow`).

**Nothing in the default game changed.** "One feud record" is a sixth rule switch, off unless ticked. It is the design council's verdict V6 (`docs/council/2026-11-22-design-council.md`). The verdict is a recommendation, and the rules are Jacob's.

## What it does

- **One record per person with you, never wiped.** Dated chapters: how the feud began, each new scar, each settlement, each time he survived you. The character sheet has a "Feud with you" section; the story chronicle titles him from his decisive chapter ("Theodorus the Escaped", "the Returned", "the Survivor", "the Bereaved").
- **Settlements pause, never erase.** When a rivalry ends (a pardon, a gift, an office, his warming to you), its rank is kept. A new wrong brings it back at that rank: "his old feud with you comes due".
- **One grievance, one feud.** A wrong already in his record can't start a new rivalry.
- **Rank from surviving you:** escaping your guards, living through his own failed blow, getting away after wounding you (with Blows can miss), coming home from exile. The second survival makes him your Nemesis, once per man.
- **Execution passes the feud on.** The dead man's record closes; his nearest kin always takes it up, at the dead man's rank, and the game says so: "X swears that Y's death will not be forgotten."
- **Political rivals stay until something decisive happens** (Expose, prison, exile, death) instead of dropping off after two quiet seasons.
- Not built: "one returning exile per city", which needs the island's cities (Phase 2). Parked.

1. **It is the strongest fix for "the same man, again and again".** In decade-long games the most times one man became your enemy falls to about 1 to 1.5, and almost no one becomes your enemy three times (0.88 a game → 0.00 for the patron). People escalate alone gets 1.7 to 1.9.
2. **Nemeses become reachable** for careful players (about 1 game in 5 or 6 instead of 1 in 15), mostly men who escaped arrest and then came home.
3. **Political rivals flicker about half as much**, because they no longer drop away after two quiet seasons.
4. **Side effect to watch: the political-rival cap.** Political rivals who never drop off hold the two places, so fewer new ones rise. That is most of why Nicoteles ends fewer reigns and why the simple bot is deposed far less (52% → 11%). The same bot is killed more often (18% → 37%), because survivors come back stronger.
5. **Escalation plus feud is worse than feud alone** on the repetition counts (careful, Standard: 1.86 against 1.42), because refused demands open feuds that then come due. If you want both, the petition ladder is the useful half of escalation; the feud record does the rest.
6. No crash or broken state in 20,000 games.

## Recommendation (Jacob decides)

Try **One feud record** together with **Warnings are clues**. If the same petition every season still bothers you, add **People escalate** as well, knowing that the "same man" count rises a little. Watch whether political rivals who never leave feel like a slow squeeze or a stuck game.
