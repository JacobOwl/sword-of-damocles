# Council and secrets: a sweep (thread 14, 2026-11-22)

Two new rule switches, both off by default: **Your council argues** (`council`) and **Secrets and leverage** (`secrets`). This report counts what they do in headless games. Nothing was tuned to history or to a target rate; the numbers are for Jacob to judge.

How it was run: `npm run sim -- --seeds 300 --switch-sweep --sets none,council,secrets,council+secrets --bots careful,patron,simple --tunings standard --scenario citadel` (and `--scenario island`), plus `npm run sim -- --seeds 300 --bot careful --switches council,secrets` for the counts. 0 crashes and 0 broken states in every run. The default game is unchanged (100 seeds, simple bot: killed 27, deposed 41, exiled 32, as before).

## What stands out

1. **Secrets make political rivals much more dangerous.** With the switch on, the patron is deposed in 52% of citadel games (30% without) and 41% of island games (20%); the careful player 6 to 9% (2 to 4%). Why: a rival who holds one of your secrets counts it as influence and rises to "Power behind the throne" (rules v2 already said leverage over you does that), and your own deeds now make secrets for them to find. Fewer men become political rivals (about half as many), but those who do stay and climb. This is the switch doing what it says; whether it bites too hard is Jacob's call. Two knobs would soften it if wanted: count a held secret as influence only while it is deadly, or let a paid blackmail demand end the hold.
2. **Blackmail is almost always refused by the bots** (paid in 2 to 10% of demands). The demands are mostly for the treasury, a key office the bots never hand over. A person playing might pay more often; the cost of refusing (the secret told to the city, a faction souring) is shown on the petition.
3. **The council makes 16 to 21 accusations a game, about half of them false, and the careful player catches only about one lie in thirty** by watching the accused (a caught liar picks a new man to accuse, so the lies keep coming). The patron rarely watches, so it is fooled less often but learns less too. Plotters lose their council seats once named (1.4 to 1.7 a game for the patron, who lets plots grow).
4. **The council alone changes little in the numbers** (the patron survives the decade a little more often, 47 to 54% citadel), which is right for a switch that is mostly information.
5. **Mutual holds work as deterrence**: a rival kept your secret to himself 0.6 to 0.8 times a game for the careful player, who collects more secrets.

## A story from one game (citadel, careful player, seed 6, both switches)

At the first council Philistus points at the brother of Demarchus, and Leptines, already plotting, points at Heloris. In summer 404 Thearides, newly seated as the kinsman, says his own brother Leptines is plotting; the watchers prove him right, and the ruler now holds Leptines' plot over him as a deadly secret. Heloris names Andron and is right again, then Nicoteles, and the ruler makes Nicoteles' oath to Leptines public. In 401 the brother of Demarchus, who has dug up the night alarm at Leontini, demands the treasury for his silence; refused, he tells the city, and later tells it too that the rumor which set Nicoteles against Thearides was the ruler's own. Thearides, the most reliable voice at the table, names Philistus in 400 and is right a third time; the purse's seat stands empty after. Aristotle the Spartan, now the sword, accuses Aristomache in 399, and the watchers find nothing. The ruler survives the decade.
