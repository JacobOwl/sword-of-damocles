# Seat: the player and story (council on units, contingents and ground, 2026-11-27)

Statement as returned by the seat (a separate AI session). Its words; not a decision.

## 1. Verdict in three lines
- Contingents with hearts is the right idea. It is the first battle mechanic that puts towns and people into the fight, and Jacob's own idea is the best sourced of the three.
- Ground is good if the player sees one plain line, "Himilcon chose the plain; his chariots like it", and never sees the table.
- The risk is that it all turns into small modifiers. A battle Jacob retells needs one named moment (who broke, who held, who fell) that leaves a mark on a town afterwards. The draft has this as "the turning moment". It should be the centre of the design, not rule 2 of 4.

## What his tests say this must fix
- "No one or nothing stood out." He read only the structured parts of the screen. "The army part was kinda fun." By season 20: "easy", "now I seem to be fully in control of everything".
- In the third test, a 50,000-man first muster flipped Leontini and Gela by size alone, and the island fell by walking. Contingents fix this directly: a big army built from unwilling towns should be brittle.

## 2. Proposals
- **P1. One turning moment per battle, always named (cheap on top of A2).** Every battle produces exactly one sentence, "[contingent] [held / broke / went over] because [reason]", picked from the worst or best heart; it is the headline. Inspired by: CK3 battle results naming knights and commanders killed or captured (as the seat understands them); King of Dragon Pass's short scene of who distinguished themselves. Passage: 13.112.3.
- **P2. Hearts as words: Steady, Wavering, Ready to leave (cheap).** Never a number. Inspired by: Battle Brothers' per-man morale states (seat "fairly but not fully sure" of the exact list).
- **P3. Each contingent led by a named man who already exists (cheap).** The head of the town's leading census house, or the faction leader; the `generalFallsOn` knob can strike a contingent leader in a lost battle and his wing breaks. Inspired by: CK3 commanders and knights dying in battle; Pendragon's knights winning Glory or dying. Passage: Leptines at Cronium (15.17.1-2), a later pattern.
- **P4. Towns remember battles (cheap to medium).** One line on the town's sheet ("Gela's men broke at the river, Summer 403") that moves the lean and that events can read. Inspired by: Dwarf Fortress legends; King of Dragon Pass clans remembering what you did.
- **P5. A battle card kept in a short "Battles" list (medium).** Place, ground, turning moment, who fell, what changed in the towns. Inspired by: Dwarf Fortress legends mode.
- **P6. Size breeds doubt (cheap).** Each contingent from a town that leans away is a chance to waver; a 50,000-man army of five unwilling towns shows two or three Wavering tokens before it marches. Passages: 14.58.1, 14.61.3. Our invention: tying the chance to the number of contingents.
- **P7. Hostages as a held object with a name (cheap).** A named person in the citadel; later a petition or a grief. Passage: 14.61.6. (Unsure of CK3's hostage rules, so not cited.)
- **P8. Refusing battle with the price shown before the click (cheap).** Passages: 14.61.3, 14.96.2.

## 3. Cut or change
- Cap tokens at five on screen; merge small bands into "others (2)".
- Hide the ground and arms table; show one net line ("The ground favours you +1: hills, and your Sicels know them"). Give "caught crossing the river" its own line.
- Drop rule 1 (wavering −1): a −1 nobody notices tells no story. Let wavering act through the turning moment and leaving.
- Carthage's contingents: yes, but watch difficulty. Every lever on Carthage makes a "far too easy" game easier; Carthage must work the same levers on you. Otherwise do the Carthage side second.
- Park siege engines: they don't touch battles, towns or story.
- The standing wish is one choice per plan; add no more pre-battle choices.
- Keep "the horsemen are the rich families", starred (13.112.3, 14.64.5).

## 4. Inventions with a better precedent
- Heart words: Battle Brothers' morale states.
- "Breaks first and takes the heaviest losses": close to CK3's pursuit or aftermath phase (exact numbers unsure).
- Republic of Rome's legions loyal to a commander fit P3: a contingent loyal to its town's first man rather than to you.
- Ground tags with no passage stay invention; only Gela's river (13.109.5) and Syracuse's marsh (14.70.5) are sourced. Keep 14.96.1.

## 5. Example battle (invented)
1. Summer 403, the river at Gela. Himilcon outmarched you and chose the plain by the river. His chariots like it; your Sicels do not.
2. Your army: the citizens (Steady), the guard (Steady), Gela's men under Athenagoras (Wavering: Gela's council fears Carthage), the horsemen under Hipparinus (Wavering: the rich families are angry).
3. The turn: Athenagoras falls at the ford, and Gela's men break and run for their walls.
4. The guard holds the line. The day ends in a bloody draw, and Himilcon does not cross.
5. After: Gela's sheet reads "its men broke at the river; its first man is dead". Gela leans away, and Hipparinus's horsemen rode home a day early.
