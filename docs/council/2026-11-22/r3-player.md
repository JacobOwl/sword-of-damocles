## The biggest risk the council has missed
Nobody has designed the island's first 30 minutes. Today season 1 already opens with about 1,400 words, two plots and two enemies before your first move. Adding a map, commissions, supply and Carthage to that start, with a guide and help text (`ui/help.ts`) written for the Citadel, means Jacob's first island game may lose him before any of our systems get a chance. The first test needs an opening that brings in one threat at a time, and a guide rewritten for the island.
