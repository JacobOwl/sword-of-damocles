## The biggest risk the council missed
**Repeated sentences.** The island will produce many more outcomes (commissions, sieges, homecomings, Carthage's moves), and each will likely be written as one fixed sentence. Seed 7's "gathers a following" appears 39 times; the island risks the same with "Leptines returns from Catana". Two fixes. First, each new kind of outcome needs several wordings, chosen by the commander's traits and his record with you. Second, add a reported count of the most repeated chronicle sentence, alongside the 100-seed check.
