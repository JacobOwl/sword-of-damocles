## The disputes. Argue these.
D1. **Map and span.** 3-4 places (devil, systems) vs 5 (narrative, player, skeptic) vs 8 from the 405 treaty map (historian). Span: 405-395 (historian, skeptic, devil) vs 398-396 (systems) vs about 399-395 / 16 seasons (narrative, player).
D2. **The hinge between court and map.** Candidates: (a) one shared pool of men, either your defense or your army (systems); (b) "Where are you?" each season, and a regent who holds the citadel while you're away (player, devil); (c) the generalship as the office every map order needs, and arming citizens to march (historian); (d) map moves cost the same three action slots, one new campaign step (skeptic); (e) away seasons (spring/summer) vs home seasons (autumn/winter) (devil); (f) a commission: send a named man with a purpose (devil). Which combination? Which do you refuse?
D3. **Supply.** Historian: supply decides wars in the sources (14.55.5, 14.96.1). Most others refuse supply chains. Is there a minimal form of supply that earns its place in the first slice?
D4. **Faction power.** Systems: the drift toward 5 erases the rise and fall Jacob asked for; replace it. Devil: 0-10 ratings are too abstract; lead with named men. What should replace drift, and how should power show?
D5. **Balance package for the first island test.** Reap what you sow (most) vs Fair fight (systems). And should there be a named "first test" preset of switches (player)?
D6. **Rivals.** A feud record per pair that only grows (narrative) vs an enmity ratchet that can be converted into debt or favour but never erased (systems) vs nemesis by escape (player, devil). Merge them into one rule if you can. Should Nicoteles' dominance be fixed or left as the city's main threat?
D7. **Carthage's inner life.** A constitution with a general "king by law" and an account at home (historian) vs an invented war party and peace party (devil, systems). And the devil's charge that building a "mirror" is smuggling a thesis into the design.
D8. **Big proposals that change Jacob's goals** (recommendations only, he decides): losing Syracuse as a turn, not the end (play on as an exile); one dice stream per system made from the seed (changes the CLAUDE.md "one seeded RNG" rule); a "story sweep" of prose summaries beside the balance sweep; a "mark this moment" playtest button; disputed numbers from rival historians. Rank these by value for cost.
