# Round 2: The Story Engineer (rebuttal)

I accept the chair's list of agreements. One amendment to it: "exile goes to a place" only makes nemeses if the exile *remembers*. That point is D6.

**D1. Map and span.**
*Final:* 405 to 395, five places: Syracuse, Leontini, Catana (or Naxos), Aetna, and the Punic west as one place (Motya). Rhegium and Carthage are **off-map refuges**: names an exile can flee to, with no simulation. That keeps the historian's best exile stories (Rhegium's asylum, 14.87.1) at almost no cost.
*Moved by:* the skeptic and the devil. Keeping the decade changes one variable at a time, and it carries over the cast and events. And the best absence story, Herbessus (14.7.6–7), falls in year one. I drop my 399 to 395.
*Not moved:* the historian's eight places. Each place needs a named person in it, or it is a dead square.
*Vote:* 405–395, five places plus two off-map refuges.

**D2. The hinge.**
*Final:* Combine (f), (b) and (d). A map order is a **commission**: a named man, a force, a purpose. It uses one of your three action slots. If you name yourself, you choose a **regent** for the citadel. Underneath, (a) supplies the numbers: the men who march leave your defense, and the screen says so. Use (c) as friction, not as a gate. You can commission without holding the generalship, but it is unlawful: it costs standing and the assembly remembers. When Nicoteles takes the generalship, the map gets harder, not closed.
*Moved by:* the devil's commission and the player's "Where are you?". Both beat my vaguer "absence" rule.
*Refuse:* (e) as a hard rule (see D3), and any separate map turn.
*Vote:* f + b + d, with a as the arithmetic.

**D3. Supply.**
*Final:* Yes to one number. An army carries "seasons of food" (systems). It drops faster in winter and on a long siege, and at zero the men desert or turn on their commander. It also gives the devil's away and home seasons without forcing them.
*Moved by:* the historian. Dionysius withdrew "because the transport of his food supplies was reduced" (14.55.5), and the Syracusans deserted over a strategy of starving the enemy (14.96.1–2).
*Refuse:* supply lines, depots, roads.
*Vote:* one food number per army.

**D4. Faction power.**
*Final:* Replace drift with power that **people carry**. A faction's power comes from its named members' offices and standing. It moves when a person defects, dies, is exiled or comes home in triumph. Gains from agenda steps fade (decay on those gains only), but not toward a fixed 5. On screen, lead with names, and show the number second: "Hipparinus and Philistus have closed ranks: the rich families are strong."
*Moved by:* the devil (named men over 0–10) and the systems seat (drift erases the rise and fall Jacob asked for). The skeptic's finding that drift works by *searching log text for " power "* is a fragility that must be fixed in PR 0, whatever replaces it.
*Vote:* replace drift, as a switch, behind named-men display.

**D5. Balance package.**
*Final:* Reap what you sow, with thread 09's partial success and clue warnings on, as a named "First island test" preset.
*Moved by:* the systems seat's point that Fair fight's failed strikes make survivors, and survivors make nemeses. True, but partial success gets survivors without coin flips.
*Vote:* Reap + partial success + clues, named preset (player's idea, strongly yes).

**D6. Rivals: one rule.**
*Merged rule:* **every pair (you and him) has one Feud record. Chapters are added to it and never removed.** A pardon, a payment or a favour is a chapter that *converts* the feud for now, and it can come due (systems' ratchet). **Surviving you is the chapter that promotes**: an escaped arrest, a failed strike, an exile and return (player, devil, systems). A grievance already in the record can't start a rivalry twice (devil). Dropping a political rival takes a decisive chapter, not two quiet seasons. The chronicle titles the record: "Hipparinus the Returned".
*Why I insist on the record:* escape-only nemeses fix the top 5% of rivals. The flicker in seed 7 (Hipparinus starts as a political rival ten times) is the other 95%, and only a record that never resets fixes it.
*Nicoteles:* don't cut him; give him peers. His road is the only cheap one, because influence comes at 2 per office for rivals against 1 for you. Let Jacob pick one of the sweep's three fixes as a switch, and have the story sweep report how many different people end reigns. A recurring villain is good; the same one every game is samey.
*Vote:* the merged rule above.

**D7. Carthage's inner life, and the thesis charge.**
*Final:* Use the historian's version: a general "king by law" (14.54.5), a senate mood, and an account at home. The account is shown by Himilcon's end (14.76.4). Justin's hundred judges is undated, so tag it as such. The devil's war party and peace party can be the senate mood's two poles, tagged invention.
*On the thesis:* the devil is right, and I concede on my own wording. I said "the mirror runs both ways". Build stakes, not a mirror. My sourced choice stays, because it is a *choice*, not a conclusion: whether to let Carthage's men escape so the Syracusans "should never find a time of ease to assert their freedom" (14.75.3). If a mirror appears in the chronicles, that is a finding for Jacob's essay, not a feature.
*Vote:* the historian's model; drop "mirror" from the build specification.

**D8. Ranked by value for cost.**
1. **Mark this moment** (player). Cheap, and every playtest becomes replayable data. Do it in the first slice.
2. **Story sweep** (mine, so discount my bias). Cheap once log entries carry thread and cause tags. It tests the gate directly.
3. **Losing Syracuse as a turn** (devil and player). The highest story value of anything proposed: the exile who returns *is* the player's nemesis arc. Costly, so it is not in the first slice. I'd recommend it to Jacob as Phase 2's stated destination, and design exile records now so it fits later.
4. **One dice stream per system** (skeptic). High value for comparing switches, medium cost. It changes a CLAUDE.md rule, so it needs Jacob's yes. If yes, do it in PR 0 and re-pin once.
5. **Disputed numbers** (historian). Cheap, but only once armies show numbers.

---

**The verdict I'd fight hardest for:** the **Feud record that never resets**, together with **thread and cause tags on every log entry**, agreed by threads 09 and 10 before either writes island code. Continuity is the one thing every retold story needs, and the game doesn't have it yet.

**My dissent, if the council goes the other way:** if it adopts escape-only nemeses and the thread 09 escalation switch, without a persistent per-pair record, I'd ask that it be recorded as a known risk. The test is simple: the story sweep should show no person starting as a rival more than twice in a decade. If that test fails, the record goes back on the table before any more island content is added.
