## The biggest risk the council missed

The war loop has a strong positive feedback and nothing in the verdicts brakes it. A victory brings plunder. The plunder pays the guard and fills supply. A paid guard means a higher defense and more men for the next victory. Once V4 removes the drift toward 5, nothing pulls a winning ruler back, so the first won campaign may make the rest of the game easy. Only supply and the commander's popularity push back.

Two things would cover this:
- **A count in the island check:** "after the first victory, did the player's defense or silver rise in most remaining seasons?" This is to report, not to tune.
- **A sink as a candidate switch:** plunder raises the army's expectations, so its pay goes up after each win. That is the period's own pattern: the mercenaries who "demanded their pay" were paid with Leontini (Diodorus 14.78.2).
