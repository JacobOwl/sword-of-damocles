**Checking the citations in V7 and V8** (paths are under `/home/user/phoenician-punic-world/sources/`):
- *14.54.5 "king by law"*: **correct on the Greek.** The saved text reads "Ἰμίλκωνα βασιλέα κατὰ νόμον καταστήσαντες" (`diodorus/sicilian-wars.greek.txt`). The Loeb English softens it to "lawfully according Himilcon sovereign power". The verdict should name the Greek as its source.
- *14.76.4 as "an account at home that can end his career"*: **inaccurate as worded.** In Diodorus no one judges Himilcon. He "passed sentence of death upon himself and starved himself to death", as penance for impiety. The tribunal is Justin 19.2.5-6, where "a hundred judges... demand of the generals... an account", and Justin gives no date. Justin 19.3.11 also has Himilcon kill himself.
- *Justin 20.5*: **correct, but narrow it to 20.5.11-13.** There Suniatus warns Dionysius "from hatred" of Hanno. He is not "the losing side". That detail is our invention, which is fine as long as it is tagged.

Diodorus puts the divine cause first; tagging sacrilege as a morale flag keeps his reading visible. Revolt from armed citizens: 14.7.6, 14.45.5, 14.64.4. Allies flipping after a defeat: 14.88.5 (Acragas and Messana after Tauromenium). Mercenaries paid in a city: 14.78.2-3.
