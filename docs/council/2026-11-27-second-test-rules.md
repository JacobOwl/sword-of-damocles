# Council on the rule questions from the second test, 2026-11-27 (thread 39)

Jacob played the Second island test and listed 30 fixes (thread 38). His words for this: "Build all and have council decide on the rule changes." So this council **decided** items 23 to 30, and its picks are built and on in the Second island test preset. Each is still a switch in `data/switches.json`, so older games replay.

Four seats (the Player, the Systems Designer, the Historian, the Devil's Advocate) read the brief (`2026-11-27/brief.md`), checked the code and wrote statements (`2026-11-27/r1-*.md`). The chair counted the votes and broke the splits. Every Diodorus quote below was checked against the saved texts in the Phoenician-Punic World repository (all found).

## The verdicts

| # | Question | Votes | Decided | Switch (on in the second test) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23 | The guard dies only in battle | 4 of 4 yes | **Built.** Battle and storming take guard and levy in proportion. Hunger, plague, desertion and ships lost take the levy; once the levy is gone the army breaks up and the guard marches home. (The Historian's twist, unpaid desertion hitting the guard first, is not built: arrears already stop the guard defending you.) | `guardBattleOnly` |
| 24 | Love slows a rival's climb | 3 yes, 1 no | **Built, narrowly.** A political rival at opinion +3 (Devoted) climbs no higher than Contender while he stays Devoted, said once in one line. He climbs again when his love cools. The Devil's Advocate's risk (marriage solves every rival) is why it stops only the last step and needs +3, not +2. | `devotedHoldsBack` |
| 25 | Retaking a town Carthage took | 3 yes, 1 off switch | **Built.** For 8 seasons after Carthage takes a town, "Carthage's men still hold it": −2 to an embassy and to its going over to your army, and no wartime +1 for a tribute town. The Systems Designer found the wartime +1 made a just-taken town *easier* to win back. Sourced: Polyxenus told Dionysius to flee "to the Campanians, whom Himilcon had left behind to guard the districts of Sicily" (Diodorus 14.8.5). The numbers are invention. | `carthageHolds` |
| 26 | Carthage's war went quiet for years | 4 of 4 yes | **Built: the cause, not a timer.** The Systems Designer traced Jacob's replay: Carthage levied the Libyans while they were already Angry, twice, which drove them into revolt; each revolt ran about 8 seasons and Carthage could not afford to quiet it. Now Carthage stops levying once the Libyans are Angry. The quiet after a defeat is itself sourced ("after a slow recovery from the disaster", 14.95.1); the Historian's idea of Carthage working by diplomacy while recalled (Magon "formed alliances with most of the Siceli", 14.90.3, about 393) is parked. | `libyaSpared` |
| 27 | The mint: friend vs yourself | split | **Built: both sides get a price.** Striking coin yourself uses none of your three actions (once a season), but each season running costs the rich one more step of mood (the Player and the Devil's Advocate on the action cost; the Historian on the rising cost). A friendly holder who strikes for you grows his following, like the generalship (the Systems Designer). Strike coin and the levy now grey out when they would bring in nothing (display, built for everyone). | `mintHabit` |
| 28 | Requests for your offices | 3 for offers, 1 for a cap | **Built: offers.** Each of the six seat petitions offers one thing back when granted, by a fixed rule: the fleet a share of profit (1 silver), the mint the wedding gifts (2 silver), the generalship the boy's company (guard +1), the treasury word of plots (an informer), the captain's post the family's payment (1 silver), the Island house his men on guard (guard +1). **The CK3-style cap is not built** (pure invention, limits the player, and no one has hoarded yet): parked. | `petitionOffers` |
| 29 | "Pay in land" with a free town | 2 no, 1 off, 1 on | **Not built.** The bug fix stands (Leontini must be your ally or yours; he gave the mercenaries "the city and territory of the Leontines", 14.78.2, when it was his). The promise you must keep, with the mercenaries seizing a town if you fail (as the Campanians took Entella and "possessed themselves of the city", 14.9.9), is parked. | none |
| 30 | Drag armies, area foraging, war ruining crops | 3 park, 1 split | **Parked.** Click a town on the map to march there, and dotted rings on the towns within 6 days of your army, are built as display fixes. The Historian's ravaging (an army in enemy land lays waste its land, and the town loses its next harvest: "ravaged the Carthaginian territory", 14.54.4) is parked beside dragging and area foraging. | none |

## Court and Families

All four: **no big change now.** Ideas, parked: links from a person's card to his family and his court (Player, Systems); one line on the main screen when something there changes (Devil's Advocate); the Families page as where marriages are arranged, and a sword warning that opens the Court with the advice of the men around you, as Heloris, Polyxenus and Philistus each advised Dionysius in 404 (Historian, Diodorus 14.8.4 to 14.8.6). The man coming for you gets his own card on the main screen (item 22), which answers the Devil's Advocate's point that the death was the worst moment and was not on the rules list.

## Risks named

- 23 and 27 make the game easier, which Jacob already called "easy" by season 20. 25 and 26 push the other way. The batch after the build counts both.
- 26 must not be tuned toward the historical dates of Carthage's returns. Count the gaps between landings and report.
