## 2. Top 3 questions
**Q1. When you play carefully, how often should Dionysius live to see 395?** (a) usually, about 2 in 3; (b) about half the time *(recommended)*; (c) rarely.

**Q2. When will you play the island test, and how much?** (a) one game on the preset by Wednesday morning, at least to the year-4 pause *(recommended)*; (b) several games comparing Standard and the preset; (c) not before Wednesday; (d) the whole decade, so the war is judged too.

**Q3. Should the game ask you to choose an ambition at the start, as well as surviving?** (a) no; (b) yes, as an option, one of three, judged by the chronicle's last page, no score *(recommended)*; (c) yes, always.
