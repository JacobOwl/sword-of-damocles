# Big scenes: how other games do it (council games report)

Thread 46, 2026-11-27. Opened with a web fetch tool that returns the page's text with quotes it marks as exact; the quotes were not re-checked by hand against the raw page, so check again before quoting elsewhere. Pages that would not load are listed at the end.

| Game | What it does | How it helps us | Quote and page |
|---|---|---|---|
| King of Dragon Pass | Scenes with numbered answers; seven advisers comment and disagree; a battle opens with a clash that shifts the odds | Council members speak in a scene; the battle's first choice moves the odds, it doesn't decide | "the initial clash is like rock/scissors/paper that affects the odds"; "Their personality may color their advice, but they are also full of good information." ([a-sharp.com/kodp/tips.html](https://a-sharp.com/kodp/tips.html)) |
| Six Ages | Scenes chosen by conditions; a choice can bring a scene back later. Battle in three phases (planning, skirmish, melee) with a balance bar each melee turn pushes | The battle in phases with a bar | "Scenes are often picked at random, though with conditions to make sure they're relevant or interesting"; "a choice may trigger another scene at a later date" ([blog, "Not Branching"](https://blog.sixages.com/index.php/2020/11/)). Battle phases: fan wiki, summary only, no quote ([sixages.fandom.com/wiki/Battle](https://sixages.fandom.com/wiki/Battle)) |
| Crusader Kings III, Struggles | A long crisis with named phases; actions ("catalysts") push toward the next; hard ending decisions | An internal crisis as phases; a scene at each change | "A struggle is a long-form conflict"; "Phases progress between each other by way of catalysts"; "we've peppered all of this with phase-specific events" ([Dev Diary 94](https://admin-forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/developer-diary/crusader-kings-3-dev-diary-94-anatomy-of-a-struggle.1522115/post-28235039)) |
| Crusader Kings III, schemes rework | Phases earn advantage; each discovery is a "breach"; too many and the scheme dies; the schemer picks when to strike | Warnings as counted breaches; the plot's last scene | "When you hit the maximum number of breaches, your scheme is automatically destroyed." ([Dev Diary 157, devtrackers copy](https://devtrackers.gg/crusader-kings/p/69618989-dev-diary-157-schemes-stories)) |
| Crusader Kings III, single combat | Up to four rounds; three moves offered each round from the fighter's skills; odds recalculated | A short duel or attempt on your life | moves come "from a pool of three options available, each time randomized based on the skills of the duelist." ([Gamepressure news](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/ck3-expanded-single-combat-system-in-patch-13/zc2ee0)) |
| Stellaris, Situations | A progress bar moves each month by your response, reasons in a tooltip; stages fire events; ends at either end | A cheap, readable frame for a crisis | "When either end of the Situation's progress bar is reached, the Situation is resolved." ([Dev Diary 245](https://admin-forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/developer-diary/stellaris-dev-diary-245-we-have-a-situation.1514701)) |
| Disco Elysium | Red checks happen once and stand; white checks can be retried later; odds shown | Battle and sword rolls as red checks with odds shown; a lost debate can be tried again next spring | "We use active dice rolls of the red check variant, where both the negative and positive outcomes are played out." ([devblog](https://discoelysium.com/devblog/2018/05/03/combat-in-disco-elysium)) |
| Griftlands | Negotiation as a duel of resolve; arguments attack and defend | Debate with standings going up and down | "The goal in negotiations is to wear the opponent's resolve to zero before the player character's." ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griftlands)); "Your attacks revolve around a "core argument," your health becomes your "resolve,"" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/griftlands-complex-double-deck-building-shows-promise-in-early-access)) |
| Republic of Rome (board game) | Persuasion by bids others can secretly counter; votes in order, blocs, a tribune's veto | The assembly vote as objects: order, blocs, veto | "Players can either vote (with possible bribes), abstain, or veto (using a tribune)." ([PBEM rules](https://mat.uab.cat/~artes/hobbies/ror/pbemrulesv4.html)) |
| The Banner Saga | No right answers, only priorities; in big battles a command, then pursue or pull back | A pursuit pivot with real risk | "there are no right choices, only priorities" ([GDC review paper](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2020/GameNarrativeReview/Benjamin%20Mora-Davison%20-%20The%20Banner%20Saga-paper.pdf)); "Choosing it will let you avoid the battle but many of your Fighters and Varl will die." ([Gamepressure guide](https://gamepressure.com/thebannersaga/warfare/ze5d14)) |
| Field of Glory: Empires | Ranged then melee; a reserve beyond the frontage; the larger side flanks; generals add dice | Backs our reserve, wings and general modifier | "a unit will get one dice roll plus one per rating of the general in charge." ([Dev Diary 9](https://www6.slitherine.com/news/field-of-glory-empires-dev-diary-9-the-battle-system29-04-19)) |
| Pentiment | You must accuse someone without being sure, and never learn if you were right | After a plot, blame someone on doubtful evidence | "it's never going to be clear if that's really the person." ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/in-obsidans-murder-mystery-pentiment-dont-expect-to-know-if-you-idd-the-right-killer)) |
| Oath (Leder Games) | The winner's map carries into the next game | A scene leaves a mark in the world | "When a game ends, the territory controlled by the winning player will form the map for the next game." ([designer diary 4](https://ledergames.com/blogs/news/oath-designer-diary-4-a-map-that-remembers)) |
| 80 Days | Hand-written content, arriving in a varied order | Hand-write the sourced scenes, vary their order | "not randomly generated but is in fact randomly authored." ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/threats-luggage-and-mild-peril-designing-i-80-days-i-)) |
| Reigns (to avoid) | Binary swipes from a weighted pool; recent cards removed | Keep the anti-repeat rule, drop the yes/no | the player is "taking binary decisions about his Kingdom." ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-creating-an-adaptive-narrative-in-i-reigns-i-)) |
| Suzerain | Ministers speak for positions | Council voices in a scene | "Every character advocated for the society they wanted to be in." ([AV Club](https://www.avclub.com/suzerain-interview)) |
| Total War | Post-battle grades | A named result over a picture | "The best possible option for winning the battle is the heroic victory." ([Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/total-war-warhammer-3/heroic-victory/z7f67d)); the other grades are general knowledge, unverified |
