## What the war game is today (read src/core/island.ts, src/core/carthage.ts, data/island.json, data/carthage.json, data/island-events.json for detail)
- Map: 8 cities + Aetna fortress, 13 routes with days' march. City: side (yours, ally, free, tributary, Carthage's, exiles), walls 0-3, men (strength points, 1 = about 5,000), grain, lean -3..3, fear 0-3. Towns have NO internal politics: one number (lean) and a side.
- Your army: one army at a time. Muster at Syracuse (citizens' levy if not Angry, 1 per ally town with men 2+, optionally the guard). Can't add men in the field. Send the army (menu of towns, by path; 20 days a season, 10 in winter; stops where an enemy army stands). Food: refills to 2 in a friendly town with 2+ grain if not besieging; else -1 a season; at 0, lose a quarter to hunger. Forage strips one town's farms. Disband.
- Battle: each side d6 + floor(men/2) + general's traits (cap 2) + own ground +1, hungry -1, guard loyal +1/unpaid -1, you lead in person. Higher wins; winner loses 1/6, loser 1/3 and retreats to nearest friendly town; tie both lose 1/6. Battle odds shown before marching. One roll, no terrain, no choices in battle.
- Sieges: arriving at a hostile walled town starts a siege; town loses grain each season (less for a port unless Carthage's fleet blockades); starve out at 0; Storm the walls: d6 + men/3 + siege lines (max 2) + engines - 2*walls - defenders/2 vs 4. Camp plague in summer/autumn. Unwalled town with half your men opens gates. Fear surrender roll. On take: sack (silver, fear in neighbours, lean -3) or spare.
- Towns going over: each season, a free/tribute town within 6 days of your army rolls d6 + lean + fear + size + strongest-army etc vs 7. Embassy to a city: one roll for 1 silver (this is how Gela was retaken for free). Carthage never garrisons what it takes. Allies give 1 silver in summer if grain 2+, and men at muster.
- Carthage: council of blocs (Elders, Hundred, people; Libyans can revolt). Each season picks a goal (rebuild, tribute, war, peace, libya) by weighted vote, then 2 acts from a priority list (trade, hire, levy, ships, fortify, general, invade, reinforce, silver to plotters, peace offer, recall, propitiate, quiet the Libyans). War when anger (0-10 clock) reaches 6/7/8 per bloc. During a Libyan revolt the goal is "libya" and the army is recalled: that is why the war went quiet for years. Peace is only "each keeps what it holds". No war aims, no war score, no demands, no indemnity. General's orders: find food, relieve a besieged town, keep a siege/storm, retake lost towns, take Messana/the straits, march on Syracuse.
- 45 island events exist (siege events, Carthage rumors, Sicel lords, Agyris, Archonides, Rhegium, Heloris, "A gate at midnight", "Pay in land" etc).
- Other systems that can be reused: the contested board (offices as seats with holders who act for/against you; city commands as small seats; src/core/board.ts), quarrels with terms, secrets, dinners, commission (who leads the army; you lead in person), convoys (grain at sea, switch), great works, exile.
- UI (ui/): a "Season" page (ledger, seats, what changed, news, dangers), "The island" tab (SVG map with dotted routes and day numbers, town marks, army shields; click a town for a sheet), actions in a separate Actions menu. Look: museum-catalogue, parchment, no dark mode. See the art target /mnt/project-files/art-pass/island-map.png and Jacob's screenshots in docs/playtests/2026-11-26-jacob-test2/*.png.
