# The opening of spring 398 BC: the card for the Near-war test

kind: history. The facts behind the late opening in `data/presets.json` (preset "Near-war test"). Quotes were opened in the Loeb Diodorus (Oldfather) saved in Jacob's Phoenician-Punic World repo (read-only) on 2026-11-27: `sources/diodorus/english/book14-1-18-97-117.loeb.txt` (14.14 to 14.18) and `book14.loeb.txt` (14.41 to 14.47).

**Dates.** Diodorus counts by archon years, so these are ranges: the Chalcidian towns about 403, the Epipolae wall about 401, the marriages and the vote for war about 398 to 397 (`calendar.md`).

**What is invention.** The season chosen (spring 398), every number (silver, grain, the guard, Carthage's anger, how long the plague lasts), Messana as a game "ally", and how warm each town is.

## What is true when the game opens

- **Aetna.** Dionysius "encamped near Aetnê and won the fortress, the exiles there being no match for an army of such size" (14.14.2).
- **Catana.** "He also meted out a similar treatment to the Catanians, selling the captives he took as booty in Syracuse ... and granted to the Campanians the city of the Catanians as their dwelling-place" (14.15.3). In the game Catana is simply yours; its Campanian settlers are not modelled.
- **Leontini.** The Leontines "assented to the proposal, left their city, and removed to Syracuse" (14.15.4). The game keeps Leontini as a town of yours.
- **The Epipolae wall.** "He decided that he must fortify Epipolae at the point where there stands now the Wall with the Six Gates" (14.18.3).
- **Messana.** "He made a present to the Messenians of a large piece of territory on their borders, binding them to him by such a benefaction" (14.44.4). The game reads this as a friendly town, an ally.
- **Greeks leaving for Carthage, and the hope of war.** "When Dionysius observed that some of the Greeks were deserting to the Carthaginian domain ... he concluded that so long as he was at peace with the Carthaginians many of his subjects would be wanting to join their defection, whereas, if there were war, all who had been enslaved by the Carthaginians would revolt to him" (14.41.1).
- **The plague.** "The Carthaginians were inactive because of the plague which had broken out among them and had destroyed the larger part of the inhabitants of Libya" (14.45.3, Dionysius' own words to the assembly, so his case for war, not a neutral report).

## Silent or weak

- Nothing in Diodorus on the treasury, the guard's size or the grain in store in 398.
- Messana's politics stayed divided (14.40, `diodorus-401-399.md`); "ally" is the game's simplification.
