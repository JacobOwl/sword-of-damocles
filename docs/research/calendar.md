# How Diodorus names the game's years

kind: history. This card backs every record in `data/calendar.json`. Each quote was searched for and found in the saved Loeb text of Diodorus in Jacob's Phoenician-Punic World repo (read-only) on 2026-11-22.

Diodorus dates each year by the archon at Athens and the Roman magistrates, and every fourth year by the Olympiad. The game uses the archon and the Olympiad only.

| Game year (from autumn) | Diodorus | Quote |
|---|---|---|
| 405 to 404 | 13.104.1 | "At the end of this year Alexias was archon in Athens" |
| 404 to 403 | 14.3.1 | "There was no archon in Athens because of the overthrow of the government ... in this year the Ninety-fourth Olympiad was celebrated" |
| 403 to 402 | 14.12.1 | "Eucleides was archon in Athens" |
| 402 to 401 | 14.17.1 | "At the close of the year Micion was archon in Athens" |
| 401 to 398 | not saved | Diodorus 14.19 to 14.40 is not among the saved texts, so these three years show no archon. |
| 398 to 397 | 14.44.1 | "Ithycles was archon in Athens" |
| 397 to 396 | 14.47.1 | "in Athens Lysiades became archon" |
| 396 to 395 | 14.54.1 | "in Athens Phormion assumed the archon ship ... and the Ninety-sixth Olympiad was celebrated" |

The Olympiad without an archon is 404/403, the year of the Thirty at Athens.

## How far to trust the match

- An Athenian archon year ran from about midsummer; the game's year starts in autumn. So each game year overlaps its archon year by about three seasons, not four.
- Diodorus is known to put some Sicilian events a year early or late. The chronicle says "Diodorus' year", not a date.
- Alexias (13.104.1) and Lysiades (14.47.1) are as spelled in this translation; other editions differ.
