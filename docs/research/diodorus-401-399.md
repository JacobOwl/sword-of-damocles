# Diodorus 14.19 to 14.40: the years 401 to 399

kind: history. Found by the source-search thread (15), 2026-11-22. Quotes are in `sources/diodorus/book14-19-40.loeb.excerpts.txt` (in this repo, not the phoenician-punic-world one). They come from Bill Thayer's LacusCurtius copy of the Loeb translation (C. H. Oldfather, vol. VI, 1954), which the page marks "The text is in the public domain."

**Only short exact quotes are saved**, not the whole chapters: the cloud's web reader returns short passages, not pages. They are enough for the gaps below.

## What these chapters say about Sicily

Almost nothing. 14.19 to 14.31 are Cyrus' march and the Ten Thousand; 14.32 to 14.39 are Athens, Sparta and Persia. The Sicilian lines:

- 14.34.3 (Messenians expelled by Sparta, about 401 to 400): "some of them, sailing to Sicily, took service as mercenaries with Dionysius".
- 14.37.5 (about 400 to 399): "Dionysius founded in Sicily a city just below the crest of Mount Aetnê" ... "and named it Adranum, after a certain famous temple." No numbers.
- 14.40 (Diodorus' year 399 to 398): Rhegium, angry that Dionysius "had sold into slavery the Naxians and Catanians, their kinsmen", and urged on by Syracusan exiles "resident in Rhegium", sends "six thousand infantry", "six hundred cavalry, and fifty triremes"; Messana's generals add "four thousand infantry, four hundred cavalry, and thirty triremes" "without obtaining a vote of the people". At Messana's border the soldiers turn home after "a harangue delivered by the Messenian Laomedon"; Rhegium turns back too; Dionysius, who had waited at "the border of the Syracusan territory", "concluded peace."

14.40 is a ready-made event (a neighbour's war that falls apart before a battle, because an assembly was skipped); for thread 06 or the island thread to use if they want.
