# Powers abroad and Carthage's return, 398 to about 393 BC (research card)

Thread 48, 2026-11-27. Used by `docs/council/2026-11-27-abroad.md` (switches `carthageReturns` and `friendsAbroad`, not built). Every quote was opened in the saved texts under `phoenician-punic-world/sources/` (files named in the council's historian statement, `docs/council/2026-11-27-abroad/historian.md`). 14.40 is in this repo, `sources/diodorus/book14-19-40.loeb.excerpts.txt`. Dates are Diodorus' archon years, so each is a range of about a year.

## Carthage after a lost army

- About 396: "they constructed ships and made careful provision of supplies for the war" (Diodorus 14.77.5).
- About 393: Magon "set about retrieving the Carthaginian cause after the disaster they had suffered, for he showed kindness to the subject cities and received the victims of Dionysius' wars. He also formed alliances with most of the Siceli" (14.90.2 to 14.90.3).
- About 393/392: "after a slow recovery from the disaster they had suffered at Syracuse, resolved to keep their hand in Sicilian affairs", with "troops from Libya and Sardinia as well as from the barbarians of Italy", "no less than eighty thousand in number" (14.95.1). The number is Diodorus'; ancient army totals are often doubted.
- About 392: peace, "the conditions were like the former except that the Siceli were to be subject to Dionysius and that he was to receive Tauromenium" (14.96.4).
- Carthage's council, 398/397: the letter "had been read in the council and subsequently before the people" (14.47.2).
- Libyan levies: Devereaux reads them as "paid conscripts" (`acoup/raising-carthaginian-armies-2.devereaux.md`); older scholars as mercenaries. Both readings stand.
